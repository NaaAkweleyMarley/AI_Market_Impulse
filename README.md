# AI_Market_Impulse
## Project Overview 
Analyzing data from **Google, Meta and OpenAI (2015 to 2024)**, to track **Innovation, Revenue, and Impact** in the field of Artificial Intelligence. 

## Motivation
Close to five years ago, I believed that *"Technology might be taking over the world soon"*. The result of that belief, however, was an imagination of a world where robots turn against human beings. My entry into the tech space has nonetheless brought me to the realization of the collaborative relationship between technology and humanity. I have embarked on several technical projects such as this, whose findings have emphasized the role of technology as a driving force for transformative action, and not a substitution of human existence. This project explores the trajectory of Artificial Intelligence through data, and was designed and presented as my **Microsoft Excel Capstone Project** in week two of my Data Analytics training with **Blossom Academy**. 

## Technology Used
### Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning (Filter, Replace, and Duplicate checks)
- Column creation (*Event_flag* for tracking AI-related events)
- Pivot analysis for KPIs
- Data visualization (line charts, donut charts, bar charts, clustered bar charts, amongst others)

## Process
### Data Cleaning
- Checked for duplicates and standardized formatting across numerical columns.
- Trearted missing values systematically using Filter and Replace functions.
- Created an *Event_flag* column to capture AI-related announcements.
### Data Visualization and Analysis
- Built KPIs (Total AI Revenue, Total Research and Development (R&D) Spending, Average AI Revenue Growth, Average Stock Impact, and Event Count).
- Designed six charts to show AI Revenue Trend, R&D Spending Share, Stock Movements on Events, R&D vs Revenue, Growth Rates, and Event Impact on Stock.

## Key Findings
- **Total AI Revenue:** $483,583 million across the three companies
- **Total R&D Spending:** $714,356 million invested into AI development (building, improving and testing AI)
- **Average AI Revenue Growth:** 159%
- **Stock Market Impact:** 2.56% of company share prices were influenced by AI-related events
- **AI-related Events:** AI-related events, product launches and announcements were recorded 10,959 times in the dataset 
  
## Dashboard Visualizations
1. **Line Chart (AI Revenue Trend):** AI revenue rises sharply from 2019, showing rapid growth.
2. **Donut Chart (R&D Spending Share):** Google leads with 59%, followed by Meta at 37% and OpenAI at 4%.
3. **Bar Chart (Stock Movements on Events):** Top impacts were the predicted release of GPT-5 (18.5%), the release of GPT-4 (15.2%), ChatGPT (12%), DALL.E 2 (9.8%) and GPT-3 (8.5%). 
4. **Clustered Bar Chart (R&D vs Revenue):** Google and Meta balance spending and returns; OpenAI invests heavily but has yet to generate equivalent returns. 
5. **Bar Chart (Growth Rates):** All three companies are growing at a very fast and almost equal pace. 
6. **Horizontal Bar Chart (Event Impact on Stock):** OpenAI's events have the biggest impact (0.041) on the market, despite the company's lower spending and revenue.
